Neel: indigo cotton

PawSetu dressed in the household's indigo: the deep blue of hand-block-printed cotton, a white with the faint blue of laundry neel, and turmeric yellow for the moments that deserve it. Calm and trustworthy for health, warm and Indian for home, and unlike any other pet app.

The signature: today, printed

Each task of the day is a small hand-block motif. Waiting tasks show the carved outline; when you log one, it prints in. The border under the day prints along as you go, and when the last task is logged the border completes and Bruno's day is done. It replaces the plain progress ring, and the same motif marks each pet everywhere.

Your five fixes, built in

  • 1Navigation stays. The bottom bar on phones and the indigo side menu on laptops stay on every screen.
  • 2"Me" in groups: Bruno and family, records and money, account.
  • 3Calmer Home: today first in the indigo band, then what's due, then one card at most.
  • 4Small celebrations: a task prints in when logged, and the border completes the day.
  • 5Readable text: bigger small text, stronger contrast, nothing cut off with "…".

Learned from the ideas set aside

  • +Every number (doses, weights, rupees, times) uses even-width figures, so columns line up.
  • +Each pet's colour and motif is identical on every screen, so a glance says whose record it is.
  • +Counts sit right-aligned at the end of each task line, so the day adds up at a glance.
  • +Motifs and text share one 8-point grid; turmeric appears only where an action or a finished day needs it.

Honest risk: indigo and turmeric must stay a working palette, not a costume. Patterns appear only as the day's motifs and pet marks, never as wallpaper.

Kolam: the morning ritual

The rice-flour drawing at the doorstep. A dark slate band, rice-white lines, turmeric for highlights. Each task logged closes one loop of the day's kolam; a finished day is a finished drawing.

Honest risk: kolam is strongest in South India and less familiar in the north; the drawing must stay readable as progress, not become decoration.

Signal: airport-sign clarity

Black on signal yellow, like airport wayfinding. One next thing at a time in huge numbers, every task a clear sign band. The clearest of the four to scan in a hurry.

Honest risk: very clear but cold for a home and a pet; yellow everywhere can feel like an alert, which fights the calm the app needs.

Standard: the familiar app look

The usual clean phone-app style: white cards, one blue colour, a progress ring. Safe and well understood, the way most apps look.

Honest risk: it looks like every other app, so PawSetu would not feel premium or its own; this is close to where it is today.

Ideas set aside

Five more styles were tried against Neel and lost on both counts that matter here: would Priya feel at home, and would the app stay clear. Each still taught Neel one thing.

Black-and-white data artToo cold and abstract for a family's pet. Kept: every number in even-width figures.
Tech product minimalismGrey with one accent, the look of any software site. Kept: turmeric only where an action or a finished day needs it.
Record-sleeve catalogueCoded colours hide meaning. Kept: each pet's colour and motif identical on every screen.
Mixtape coverHandwritten charm, but hard to read. Kept: counts right-aligned at the end of every task line.
Grid type specimenA poster, not a home. Kept: motifs and text on one 8-point grid.
