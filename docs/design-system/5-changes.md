# Changes from today's app

Everything else in this system is the app as it is today, written down. These are the places where the system asks for something different. None of them is in the app yet; each one waits for the owner's OK. They keep the current look (teal, apricot, Plus Jakarta Sans, soft cards) and do not bring in anything from the "Neel" proposal.

| # | Change | Why | Where in code | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Smallest reading text goes from 13 to 14 px.** `bodySmall` 13 → 14 (`support`), `labelMedium` 13 → 14 (`label-sm`: chips, nav labels), `labelSmall` 12 → 13 (`caption`). | Weakness 5 in the app review: important details sit in 13 px grey. `bodySmall` is used 242 times in feature code. This is the planned "bigger readable text" step. | `app_theme.dart` text theme; recheck the layout sweep test | Small |
| 2 | **Control borders use `outline-strong` (#798A8C).** Text fields, outlined buttons, unselected chips, checkboxes. | Today's border `outline` (#E5E1DA) is 1.3:1 on white, below the 3:1 WCAG asks for control edges. High-contrast mode already strengthens it; normal mode should too. | `tokens.dart` (new field), `app_theme.dart` input, outlined button and chip themes | Small |
| 3 | **Tonal buttons are teal by default.** Apricot tonal only in Shop and celebrations. | Flutter's tonal button takes the secondary colour, so all 12 tonal buttons are apricot, including "Health report" and "Medical card" on the Health tab and buttons in the document vault and tele-vet, all clinical screens. | New `TonalButton` in `lib/core/widgets/buttons.dart` with a `warm` option; replace `FilledButton.tonal` | Small |
| 4 | **Three card tiers:** Hero (tinted, no shadow, one per screen), Standard (today's card), Quiet (`surface-low`, no shadow). | Weakness 3: Home shows many equal white cards, so nothing says "start here". | `PawCard` gets a `tier` option; Home uses Hero for the pet card, Quiet for weather and memory | Medium |
| 5 | **Navigation stays visible on detail screens.** Only focused tasks hide it: camera, video call, SOS sheet, walk tracker, booking and checkout steps. | Weakness 1, and planned improvement 1. | `lib/core/routing/extra_routes.dart`: move detail routes inside the shell | Medium |
| 6 | **Small celebrations** with `duration-celebrate`: last checklist item, vaccine recorded, streak milestones. | Weakness 4, and planned improvement 4. Not on Health's clinical screens. | `ProgressRing`, Today card, vaccine save | Medium |
| 7 | **Three page widths** (`width-narrow` 560, `width-reading` 760, `width-max` 1200) instead of the ten in use today (420, 560, 640, 720, 760, 800, 820, 900, 1000, 1100). | Pages of the same kind should line up when you move between them on the web. | `PageBody` / `ContentWidth` callers | Small |
| 8 | **Six avatar sizes** (24 inline, `avatar-chip` 28, `avatar-sm` 40, `avatar-md` 56, `avatar-hero` 76, `avatar-profile` 132) instead of 16 different sizes. | Same reason: one pet should look the same size on similar screens. | `PetAvatar` callers | Small |
| 9 | **Fixed line heights per style** (table in **Typography in three scripts**) instead of one multiplier (1.4 English, 1.6 Hindi and Telugu). | Large titles at 1.4 are loose (32 px gets 45 px lines); body at 1.4 is a little tight. Lines that sit on the 4 px grid also make spacing predictable. | `app_theme.dart` `TextStyle(height:)` per style | Small |
| 10 | **`headline` is 26 px**; stop using 24, 28 and 36. Slight negative letter-spacing on English sizes 22 and up only. | 22 and 24 are too close to tell apart; 26 fills the gap between `title` 22 and `display` 32. | `app_theme.dart` | Small |
| 11 | **Easy mode spacing** grows too: card padding 24, gaps 16, icon-to-label 12. | Today Easy mode enlarges text and targets but keeps the same gaps, so screens feel crowded at 125% text. | `Space` helpers read Easy mode | Medium |
| 12 | **In high contrast, `primary` text uses `teal-800`** (#115E59, 7.2:1). | Links and text buttons are the only text below 7:1 in high-contrast mode (5.2:1 today). | `AppTheme.build` when `highContrast` | Small |
| 13 | **Discount badges on product photos use `success-container` with `success` text.** | A solid green badge with white text only works in light mode: in dark mode `success` is a light green and white text on it fails. | `shop` product card | Tiny |
| 14 | **SOS label weight 700**, not 800. | Weight 800 is not bundled, so it already renders as 700; saying 700 keeps code and design the same. | `sos_button.dart` | Tiny |

Additive tokens that change nothing on screen: `space-xxs`, `space-3xl`, `space-4xl`, `radius-sm`, `radius-inner`, `accent-text`, the `*-container` status colours and the full colour ramps.
