# Demo pictures

Since 15 Nov 2026 (UI Design 6) the demo uses no photos. The pets, the album pictures and the shopfronts of clinics, groomers and other services are drawings from the UI Design 6 design boards (`docs/design-proposals/2026-11-10-toran-redesign/source/kit.js`), drawn for this project. They are rendered to PNG files in `assets/images/demo/` by `tools/drawings/render.cjs` and made smaller by `tools/drawings/shrink.py`.

The public-domain (CC0) photos used before then, and where they came from, are listed in this file's Git history (before commit `a52f2c4`, which removed them).
